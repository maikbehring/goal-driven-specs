# Examples — inspiring Mini-visions + one result per KR

Copy the **feel**. A stranger should understand and want it.  
**Rule:** each Key Result measures **exactly one** outcome (no “and/or” joining two wins).

## Consumer trip wallet

**Goal 1 — Strong alone (active)**  
**Mini-Vision:** On trips you always know who paid what — easy as a photo, no note chaos.  
**How we know:**
1. **The split feels fair.** At least one solo trip ends with a settlement that feels clear and fair.
2. **Value before signup.** Fresh start: you save the first expense before the app asks you to sign up.
3. **You’d invite.** Self-score ≥8/10 on “Would I invite my partner now?”  
   *(Tip: score after you’ve really used the app on a trip.)*

**Goal 2 — Together on the road (next)**  
**Mini-Vision:** Friends share a trip wallet on their phones that they’re happy to use on the next trip.  
**How we know:**
1. **Five people arrive.** Five people open the app after a normal install (not a special developer tool).
2. **Their expense shows up fast.** At least one shared trip where the other person’s expense is visible within five minutes.
3. **Next trip again.** At least four of five people asked want to use it on the next trip (score ≥4 of 5).

## B2B SaaS

**Goal 1 — Drop the spreadsheet (active)**  
**Mini-Vision:** Your team runs the weekly job here — the spreadsheet gathers dust.  
**How we know:**
1. At least N teammates use the core flow every week.
2. A typical core-job run takes ≤ Y minutes.
3. At least N teammates say “use this, not the sheet.”

**Goal 2 — Partners in week one (next)**  
**Mini-Vision:** Outside teams get clear value without hand-holding on a call.  
**How we know:**
1. Three partner spaces finish setup alone.
2. Three partners finish the core job within seven days.
3. Three written “would continue.”

## AI feature

**Goal 1 — Prefer the assistant (active)**  
**Mini-Vision:** People choose the AI path because it is faster and they trust it.  
**How we know:**
1. Share of sessions that finish with AI without a manual redo: X% → Y%.
2. Slow happy-path cases: A seconds → ≤ B seconds.
3. Dogfood score “prefer AI vs typing it myself” ≥ 8/10.

**Goal 2 — Safe for many (next)**  
**Mini-Vision:** Lots of people can use it without support drowning.  
**How we know:**
1. Quality gate failures: N → 0.
2. Support tickets per 1 000 AI sessions: X → ≤ Y.
3. Policy problems in an audit sample: N → 0.

## Compound → single result

| Two results (bad) | One result (good) |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Install **and** open | People who **open** after a normal install |
| Eight expenses in-app **and** invite score ≥8 | Invite score ≥8 *(usage tip in a note, not a second KR)* |
| Each adds an expense **and** sees the other in 5 min | Other person’s expense visible within 5 min |
| “Would continue **or** would pay” | Pick one: “would continue” |
